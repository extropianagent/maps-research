# Curators

Public Google Maps lists and profiles worth returning to, with the owner's own
judgement of each. Only public links and the owner's words: no review text, no
personal details beyond a public name, no copies of a list's contents.

A lead becomes trusted when the owner says so, usually after visiting places it
led to. Unknown is a valid entry; fill it in when evidence arrives.
