# Feedback log

What the owner thought of places they actually went to or people they actually
hired. This is the evidence preferences are learned from, so it holds only the
owner's own words and scores, with place IDs and links. No review text, ratings
or addresses from Maps.

Add a row whenever the owner reports back on a place the toolkit suggested, or
mentions a visit worth remembering.

| Date | Place ID | Maps link | Domain | Occasion or job | Owner's score (0-10) | Owner's words | Suggested by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Learning from it

After every fifth new row, read the last five against `preferences.md` and
propose at most two changes to the preferences: something to add, sharpen or
drop. Show the owner the rows each proposal rests on. Change `preferences.md`
only for the proposals the owner accepts, recording the date and their words.

Scores are not averaged. A 9 for a quick lunch and a 9 for an anniversary
dinner say different things; the occasion column is what makes a score usable.
