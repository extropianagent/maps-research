# Hiring: tradespeople, contractors, local services

Read after the brief in `../SKILL.md`. Objective `hire`. This adds what is
specific to choosing someone to do work.

## Search
- Many tradespeople have no shopfront. Add `--service-area-businesses` so
  businesses that only travel to customers are included.
- Query the trade and the job: "tile installer" and "bathroom renovation", the
  material, and the local terms for both.

## Filters
Apply the owner's numbers exactly. When they give none, do not invent a rating
floor: a trade with few listings loses its honest small operators that way.

## Evidence (required, with integrity)
- Word sets. Quality: the trade's own vocabulary (for tiling: "level", "grout",
  "cuts", "finish"), plus "on time", "clean", "quote". Failure: "no show",
  "late", "abandoned", "deposit", "redo", "leak", "uneven", "cracked",
  "overcharged", "stopped answering".
- Read owner replies to bad reviews. A specific, calm reply is a good sign; a
  reply that attacks the customer is a bad one.
- Integrity weighs more here than anywhere: single-review authors, bursts and
  repeated text are how a small trade listing is padded. Use
  `maps reviews contributors RUN` to see reviewers shared between competitors.

## Report
Never present a tradesperson as vetted. Five reviews cannot show workmanship.
End with what the owner should ask each pick for: photos of finished jobs,
two past customers to call, a written quote.

## Preferences worth recording
The job, budget, past tradespeople by place ID with the owner's verdict.
