# Exploring a city or neighbourhood

Read after the brief in `../SKILL.md`. Objective `explore`. This adds what is
specific to open-ended discovery.

## Plan before searching
- One run per theme (food, shops, culture, outdoors), not one giant query. A
  mixed query returns whatever is most prominent.
- See what the place has a lot of before choosing themes: `maps census` counts a
  type in an area for one cheap request. An unusual concentration is a lead.
- Work by neighbourhood with `--near` when the city is large. Whole-city
  searches favour the centre.

## Search
- Describe a feeling to Google's side as well:
  `maps discover "quiet courtyard cafes in <district>" --into RUN`.
- Rank with `explore` so one type does not fill the list; switch to `gems`
  inside a theme once it is chosen.

## Evidence
Optional at this stage. Fetch reviews only for places the owner is likely to
visit, and look for the one failure that ruins a visit: "closed", "moved",
"tourist trap", "queue", "cash only".

## Report
Group by neighbourhood, then theme, so the picks can be walked. Mark each as
well known or overlooked. Leave out anything already in the owner's known places.

## Preferences worth recording
Interests, pace, how they get around, what they have already seen.
