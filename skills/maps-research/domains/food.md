# Food: restaurants, cafes, bakeries, a particular dish

Read after the brief in `../SKILL.md`. This adds what is specific to food.

## Keywords
- The dish is a query of its own, beside the kind of place: "kunafa", "sourdough",
  "birria", in the local script too. Places known for one thing are often listed
  under a generic category.
- Add the format when it matters: bakery, stall, takeaway, counter, family
  restaurant. Leave out "best": it changes nothing.

## Filters and ranking
- Do not filter by `--type` at first. Google's primary type is often wrong here
  (a bakery listed as a cafe or a store). Filter only if the results are polluted.
- Add `--open-only`: food places close and reopen under new owners.
- "Hidden", "overlooked", "local": rank with `gems`. Otherwise `best`.
- A high review count near a mall, a landmark or on a delivery app measures
  footfall. Treat it as weak evidence of quality either way.

## Evidence
- The owner asked about food, so separate food from service: a complaint about a
  slow waiter does not count against the bread.
- Word sets for review search. Quality: the dish, plus "fresh", "crust",
  "texture", "made to order", "from scratch". Failure: "stale", "reheated",
  "frozen", "bland", "oily", "small portion", "hygiene", "sick".
- Kitchens change. Weigh recent reviews above old ones, and say when the recent
  ones disagree with the rating.

## Report
For each pick, name what to order when the reviews support it, and say so when
they do not.

## Preferences worth recording
Cuisines and dishes, dietary rules, price comfort, noise and seating, what made
a past place a disappointment.
